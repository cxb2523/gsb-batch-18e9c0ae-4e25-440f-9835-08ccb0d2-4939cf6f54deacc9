Toontown on mobile and PC

Toontown: Anywhere is the classic Toontown Online game, free to play on mobile phones and tablets as well as on computers. It runs the whole game in your browser, with touch controls made for phones and tablets, and keyboard and game controller support. Make a toon, train your gags, fight Cogs with friends, go fishing, and decorate your estate.

Get the app

Pick how you want to play. The app is the same game as the website, with the same account, settings and chat. Everything here is free and needs no app store.

Good to know

  • The first start downloads the game files (about 390 MB), so use Wi-Fi. They stay on your device after that.
  • Pick a name and a password, then choose Play. Use the same ones next time, on any device.
  • Stuck on a task? The quest guide lists every Toontask and where to go.

Questions

Can I play Toontown on my phone or computer?

Yes. Toontown: Anywhere runs in your web browser on iPhone, iPad, Android phones and tablets, and on Windows and Linux computers, with touch controls built for small screens. Open toontown.puffsco.com/play, make a toon and play.

Is there a Toontown mobile app, and do I need to download it?

No download is needed: the game runs in Safari or Chrome, and you can add it to your Home Screen so it opens full screen like an app. Free mobile and desktop apps are also available for Android, Windows, Linux and Steam Deck, and for iPhone and iPad through SideStore. None of them need an app store.

Is it free?

Yes. Toontown: Anywhere is free to play, with no purchases.

Can I play with friends on other devices?

Yes. Phones, tablets and computers all play on the same server with the same account, so you can team up with friends on any device.
